# Research roadmap

## SG-001 · Research and architecture

Established the comparison of communication formats, candidate semantics and evaluation approach. The [ADR](decisions/ADR-001.md) remains proposed.

## SG-002 · Offline pilot

The [harness](experiments/SG-002-PILOT.md) supports concise prose, structured SIL and direct A2A-compatible domain reports on one offline carrier. Deterministic verification is complete. The [48 model trials](experiments/SG-002-RESULTS.md) are blocked pending two verifiable local model families and an inference runtime.

## Proposed SG-003 · Follow-up after model evidence

First complete the existing 48-trial matrix when an eligible pair is available. Review case-level mistakes, including unsafe dispatch attempts, before choosing a new experiment. Use the paired outcomes, errors by direction, and measured machine seconds to design and cost a separately approved held-out study. For an adoption comparison, use a matched native A2A binding, independent scenarios and blind audit. If direct A2A or prose performs comparably at lower total cost, keep the simpler format and revisit which semantic distinctions merit shared conventions.
